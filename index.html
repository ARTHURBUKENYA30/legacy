Who We Are

A bigger future starts on court.

Legacy Rackets Academy uses squash as a pathway to confidence, discipline, opportunity and community. We support young people as they learn the game, build their skills and discover what they can achieve.

From first-time players to athletes working toward competitive goals, our aim is to create a welcoming route into the sport and connect promising players with the coaching, encouragement and support they need.

Helpful Answers

Good to know.

A few practical answers for players, parents and supporters. If your question is not covered, contact the academy directly.

How do I register an athlete?

Open the Join Us section and choose Register Athlete. Share the player's details and programme of interest. The academy team will follow up to discuss suitability, schedules and next steps.

Which programme is right for me?

Use the registration form to tell us the athlete's age, experience and goals. The team can advise on the most suitable available pathway. Programme availability should be confirmed directly with the academy.

Where can I find fees and training schedules?

Current fees and schedules are not listed here as fixed figures. Please contact the academy before making plans or payments so you receive the latest confirmed information.

How does a donation or sponsorship work?

Submit a donation pledge or sponsorship enquiry. The academy will respond with current options and instructions. The website does not process card payments, and you should verify the recipient and payment details before sending money.

How is my information used when I submit a form?

Forms are submitted through FormSubmit and sent to the academy email address. Please provide only the information needed to respond to your enquiry, and avoid including sensitive personal or medical details in open text fields. Contact the academy if you have a question about a submission.

Can a school or organisation partner with the academy?

Yes, you can use the sponsorship enquiry form to introduce your organisation and describe the type of partnership or support you have in mind. The team will follow up to discuss what may be possible.
