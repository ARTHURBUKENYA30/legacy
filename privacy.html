Privacy & personal information

Privacy notice

Uganda Legacy Rackets Academy respects the privacy of athletes, parents, guardians, volunteers and supporters. This notice explains the intended handling of information sent through this website. Please review it with the academy team before treating it as a final, legally approved policy.

Contact for privacy questions
ugandalegacyracketsacademy@gmail.com

Information you may provide

Depending on the form you choose, you may submit names, an athlete's age, a parent or guardian's name, contact details, programme interests, an organisation name, a sponsorship enquiry or other information you choose to include.

Why information is requested

  • To respond to registration, programme and training enquiries.
  • To discuss sponsorship, donations or partnership opportunities.
  • To follow up on requests and coordinate next steps.

Children's information

Where an enquiry concerns a child, a parent or legal guardian should complete the form. Do not include medical details, identity documents, school addresses or other sensitive information in the open text fields unless the academy has specifically explained why it is necessary and how it will be protected.

Form processing and third parties

The website's enquiry forms are configured to use FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, to forward submissions to the academy's email address. That service may process submitted information to deliver the message and is subject to its own terms and privacy practices. Do not submit information you are not comfortable sending through an external service. The academy should periodically verify that this integration remains active and appropriate.

Sharing, storage and retention

Information should be accessed only by people who need it to respond to the enquiry, and retained only as long as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. The academy should not sell enquiry information. Please contact the academy to ask about a submission, request a correction, or raise a concern. Any request will be handled subject to applicable law and the records the academy can identify.

Security and your choices

Use a personal email address or phone number you control, avoid sending unnecessary details, and contact the academy if you think information has been sent to the wrong recipient. No website or email transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the academy's practices and services change. The academy should add an effective date and review this notice before launch to ensure it accurately describes actual operations and complies with applicable Ugandan data protection requirements.

This page is a practical website notice, not legal advice. It should be reviewed and formally approved by the academy before publication as its official policy.
