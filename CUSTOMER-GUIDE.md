# How to Update Your Website

You don't need any technical skill for this — just a web browser.

## 1. Log in

Go to: **yoursite.com/admin** (your developer will give you the exact link)

The first time, you'll get an email invite — click it, set a password.
After that, just log in with your email and password whenever you want
to make a change.

## 2. Find what you want to change

You'll see one page called **"Edit Website"**. Inside it, sections like:

- Homepage Banner — your title, subtitle, and the video/photo behind it
- Featured Athletes — photos and stats for your athletes
- Photo Gallery — add or remove photos, sorted into Training,
  Tournaments, Schools, Community
- Impact Numbers, Programs, Projects, Testimonials
- Contact Info — email, phone, WhatsApp, social links

Click any section to open it.

## 3. Make your change

- **To change text:** click into the box and type.
- **To change a photo or video:** click the image/video box, then
  **"Choose an image"** (or drag a new file in) to upload from your
  computer or phone.
- **To add something** (a new athlete, a new gallery photo, a new
  testimonial): scroll to the bottom of that list and click
  **"Add [item]"**.
- **To remove something:** click the trash/delete icon next to that item.

## 4. Publish

When you're happy with your changes, click **"Save"**, then
**"Publish"** (top right). Your website updates automatically —
usually within 30–60 seconds.

## Tips

- **Photos:** for best results, use photos that aren't huge files
  (under 5MB each) — phone camera photos are usually fine.
- **Video:** the homepage background video should be an MP4 file,
  ideally under 20MB so the page loads quickly for visitors.
- You can always undo a mistake by editing the field again and
  re-publishing — nothing is permanent until you hit Publish.
- If something looks broken after a save, check that you didn't leave
  a field completely empty — most fields expect at least some text.

If you ever want a new type of section added (not just edited), or the
overall look changed, reach out to your developer for that part.
